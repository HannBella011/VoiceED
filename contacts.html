Emergency Support

For urgent matters requiring immediate attention, reach out through our emergency channels.

Note: We do not provide specific emergency numbers here, as they vary by country and location. Please use your local emergency hotline for immediate assistance.

Specialized Global Hotlines for Students

  • Find a Helpline (International Directory) – Connects students worldwide to local crisis hotlines for abuse, crime, and emotional support.
  • TeenHelp Hotlines – Covers substance use, peer pressure, bullying, and abuse, accessible 24/7.
  • UNODC Youth Initiative – Empowers students globally to report drug-related issues, crime prevention, and peer-led safety programs.
  • UNODC Global Programs – Supports youth in crime prevention, anti-drug campaigns, and safe community building.

Student-Specific Safety Channels

  • Campus Security Hotlines – Most universities maintain dedicated student safety hotlines for reporting drugs, crime, or harassment.
  • Anonymous Reporting Tools – Platforms like VoiceED allow students to report issues without fear of exposure.
  • Youth NGOs & Peer Networks– Many student organizations partner with local police and UNODC to run awareness campaigns and provide confidential peer support.

Risks & Action Steps

  • Immediate Danger: Call local emergency services (911/999)
  • Mental Health Crisis: Contact crisis hotlines immediately
  • Cyberbullying: Document evidence, report, seek support
  • Physical Threat: Move to safe location, contact authorities
  • Substance Abuse: Seek medical help, call recovery hotlines
  • Academic Pressure: Reach out to counselors, use stress resources

Actionable Advice for Students

  • 1. Memorize your local emergency number
  • 2. Use Find a Helpline for international crisis support when abroad
  • 3. Engage with UNODC Youth Initiative for drug prevention and crime safety programs
  • 4. Leverage campus security & anonymous reporting tools for immediate student-focused protection

VoiceED Description

VoiceED is a student‑driven platform that empowers learners to be heard, supported, and inspired. It promotes safety, wellness, and collaboration in schools and communities through features that let students report concerns, access mental health and motivation resources, share study materials, and find inspiration from peers. Its goal is simple: to make education inclusive and supportive by combining technology with compassion—creating a space where every voice matters and learning becomes a shared journey of growth.
